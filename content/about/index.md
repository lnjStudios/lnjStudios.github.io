**Links & Joints Studios** started as a name for the robots and tools I make outside my day job, and grew into a place to share them. It's a studio in the workshop sense: one bench, one person, and whatever is being built on it this month.

The name comes from robotics itself. A robot description is just *links* and *joints*: rigid parts, and the way they move against each other. Everything else is built on top of that.

The easiest way to reach me is [GitHub](https://github.com/koustavbetal) (issues on any of the tools are welcome) or [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/koustavbetal).
